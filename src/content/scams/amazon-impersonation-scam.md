title: The Amazon impersonation scam
aliases: ["fake Amazon order call", "Amazon refund scam", "Amazon account locked email", "Amazon Prime renewal scam"]
definition: The Amazon impersonation scam is a call, text or email about a large order you did not place, a locked account or a Prime renewal, designed to get you to call a number or click a link where a fake agent takes your login, your card, or remote control of your device.
channels: [phone, email, text]
targets: [everyone, parents]
impersonates: ["Amazon", "Amazon Prime", "Amazon security"]
redFlags:
  - A call or robocall about an iPhone or laptop order you did not make, with "press 1 to cancel."
  - An email saying your account is locked and you must verify by clicking a link.
  - A Prime renewal at a price you did not agree to, with a number to call.
  - An agent who asks you to install an app, read a code, or buy gift cards to "fix" the account.
whatToDo:
  - Do not press 1, click, or call the number given.
  - Open the Amazon app or type amazon.com yourself and check Your Orders. If nothing is there, nothing happened.
  - Forward suspicious emails to stop-spoofing@amazon.com and report at amazon.com/reportascam.
ifYouPaid:
  - Change your Amazon password and turn on two-step verification; check for unknown addresses and payment methods in the account.
  - Call your card issuer about any charge; remove any remote-access app that was installed.

Amazon is the most impersonated company in the country because nearly everyone has an account and nearly everyone is expecting a package. The hook is a charge you did not make: "Your order for a MacBook Pro, $2,149, has shipped. If you did not place this order, press 1." Pressing 1 connects you to a "security agent" who needs to "secure your account," which means your password, a code, or remote access to your phone.

The email version says the account is locked and must be verified by clicking. The link opens a copy of Amazon's login page.

Every version is defeated the same way: ignore the message, open the real app yourself, and look at Your Orders.
