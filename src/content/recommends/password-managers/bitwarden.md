## Who it is for

The person who wants the protection without the subscription, or who wants to see the code. Bitwarden's emergency access is the feature we wish every manager had: name your spouse or child, set a waiting period, and they can get in if you cannot say no.

## Setting it up for a parent

Same recipe as any manager: four-word master password in the notebook, face or fingerprint unlock, the important logins moved in during one sitting, and you set as the emergency contact with a short waiting period.
