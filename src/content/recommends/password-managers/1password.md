## Who it is for

The household that wants one answer. If you are the family's IT department, 1Password lets you set up Mom, your spouse and your college kid on one plan, share the logins that should be shared, keep the rest private, and recover anyone who locks themselves out.

## Setting it up for a parent

Install the app on their phone, create their account inside your family plan, set a four-word master password written in their notebook, and turn on face or fingerprint unlock so they almost never type it. Move their email, bank, phone carrier and Medicare logins in during the same sitting. Make yourself an organizer.

## What we would change

The price, and a simpler timed emergency-access option for people outside the family plan.
