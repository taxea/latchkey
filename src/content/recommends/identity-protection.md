## The honest version

The best identity protection is free: freeze your credit at Equifax, Experian and TransUnion, get an IRS Identity Protection PIN, claim your Social Security account, and turn on transaction alerts at your bank. [That takes thirty minutes.](/protect/identity-theft/)

Paid services add monitoring (alerts when your details show up somewhere), restoration help (a person who makes the calls if it happens), and insurance for some recovery costs. That is worth paying for in two cases: for a parent who will not manage alerts, and for anyone who would rather pay than do the thirty minutes.
