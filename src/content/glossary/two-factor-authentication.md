term: Two-factor authentication
definition: "Two-factor authentication (2FA) is a second check at login, usually a code from an app or a text, so that a stolen password alone is not enough to get in."
example: "After typing your password, you approve the login in an authenticator app on your phone."
whyItMatters: "Turn it on for email, bank and phone carrier accounts. Prefer an authenticator app or passkey over text codes, which can be intercepted by a SIM swap."
