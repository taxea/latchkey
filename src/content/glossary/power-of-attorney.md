---
term: Power of attorney
definition: "A power of attorney is a legal document that lets a person you choose act for you in financial or legal matters; a durable one stays in effect if you become incapacitated."
example: "Your daughter paying your bills and talking to your bank while you are in the hospital."
whyItMatters: "Without it, your family cannot act for you without going to court. Every adult should have one, and every adult child should know where their parents' is."
