---
term: Credit freeze
definition: "A credit freeze is a free setting at each of the three credit bureaus that blocks anyone from opening a new account in your name until you lift it."
example: "Freezing your file at Equifax, Experian and TransUnion so a stolen Social Security number cannot be used to open a card."
whyItMatters: "It is the single most effective identity-theft protection and it costs nothing; it does not affect your credit score or your existing accounts."
