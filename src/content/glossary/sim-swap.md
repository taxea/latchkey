---
term: SIM swap
definition: "A SIM swap is an attack in which a scammer convinces your phone carrier to move your number to their phone, so that they receive your calls and text-message login codes."
example: "Your phone suddenly loses service; within an hour your bank and email passwords are reset using codes sent to \"your\" number."
whyItMatters: "A SIM swap defeats text-based two-factor authentication. Add an account PIN and port-out lock at your carrier, and use an authenticator app or passkey instead of text codes."
