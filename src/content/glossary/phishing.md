---
term: Phishing
definition: "Phishing is a fake email, text or website designed to look like a company you trust so that you will enter a password, card number or personal details."
example: "An email that looks like it is from your bank says your account is locked and asks you to log in through a link. The page is a copy; the login goes to the scammer."
whyItMatters: "Almost every account takeover starts with a phishing message. The defense is never to log in through a link; go to the site yourself."
