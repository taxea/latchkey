term: Offer in Compromise
definition: "An Offer in Compromise is an IRS program that lets a taxpayer settle a tax debt for less than the full amount when the IRS agrees it could not collect more."
example: "A retiree on Social Security with an old $40,000 balance and no assets settling for an amount the IRS formula produces."
whyItMatters: "It is real and it is narrow. The IRS accepts offers that match its formula, not stories; \"pennies on the dollar\" ads are selling, not advising."
