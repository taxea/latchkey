---
term: Gift card payment
definition: "Gift card payment is the demand that you pay a debt, fee or fine by buying gift cards and reading the numbers over the phone; it is the signature of a scam because the money is untraceable and irreversible."
example: "\"Go to Walgreens, buy four $500 Apple gift cards and read me the codes.\""
whyItMatters: "No government agency, utility, company or legitimate person is paid in gift cards. The request is the proof."
