---
term: Credential stuffing
definition: "Credential stuffing is when attackers take email-and-password pairs leaked in one breach and try them automatically on thousands of other sites."
example: "A password leaked from an old forum account in 2019 is used to log in to your streaming, shopping and email accounts today."
whyItMatters: "It only works when passwords are reused. A password manager with a unique password per site makes it useless."
