term: Smishing
definition: "Smishing is phishing by text message: a text that pretends to be a toll agency, delivery company or bank and links to a fake page that collects your details."
example: "\"USPS: your package is on hold, confirm your address here.\" The link opens a copy of the carrier's site."
whyItMatters: "Texts are the fastest-growing scam channel because people trust them more than email. Turn on your phone's unknown-sender filter and never tap a link in a text you did not expect."
