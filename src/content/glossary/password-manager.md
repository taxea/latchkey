---
term: Password manager
definition: "A password manager is an app that creates, stores and fills a different strong password for every account, locked behind one master password or your device's face or fingerprint unlock."
example: "1Password, Bitwarden or the one built into your browser or phone."
whyItMatters: "Reusing one password everywhere means one breach opens every account. A manager makes unique passwords effortless and can share emergency access with a family member."
