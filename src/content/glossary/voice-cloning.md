---
term: Voice cloning
definition: "Voice cloning is the use of AI to reproduce a specific person's voice from a short audio sample, so that a scam call can sound like a family member."
example: "A call that sounds like your grandson, made from a clip of his graduation speech on Instagram."
whyItMatters: "A familiar voice is no longer proof of anything. Agree a family word and always call back on a known number."
