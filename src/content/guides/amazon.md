title: Lock down your Amazon account
service: Amazon
category: accounts
atRisk: Amazon has your cards and your address, and it is the most impersonated company in the country.
totalTime: PT10M
checklist:
  - A unique password from your password manager.
  - Two-step verification with an authenticator app or a passkey.
  - Review saved payment methods and addresses; remove old ones.
  - Review devices and apps with access; sign out of what you don't recognize.
  - Know that Amazon never calls about an order you didn't place.
steps:
  - name: Open Login & security
    text: Go to amazon.com, Account, Login & security. Everything below is here.
  - name: Change the password
    text: Password, Edit. Use a long unique phrase saved in your password manager.
  - name: Turn on two-step verification or a passkey
    text: Two-Step Verification, Edit, and choose an authenticator app rather than text. If you see Passkey, add one; it uses your phone's face or fingerprint and cannot be phished.
  - name: Review devices
    text: Login & security, Secure your account (or Your devices). Sign out of any device you do not recognize.
  - name: Clean up payments and addresses
    text: "Account, Your Payments: remove old cards. Account, Your Addresses: remove any you do not recognize; a scammer who adds an address can ship to it."
  - name: Review apps with access
    text: Login & security, Apps and websites you've given access to. Remove anything you no longer use.
forParent:
  - Turn on the passkey so there is nothing to type and nothing to phish.
  - "Show them Your Orders and the rule: a call or email about an order is checked only here, never by calling back."
  - Consider Amazon Household so you can see orders if they want you to.
recovery:
  - Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page; the reset goes to the email on the account, so secure email first.
  - If the email on the account was changed, contact Amazon customer service through the real site's Help pages, not a number from a search result.
tools: [a password manager, your phone]
related: [gmail, iphone]
scams: [amazon-impersonation-scam, package-delivery-scam]
faq:
  - q: I got a call about an Amazon order I didn't place. What do I do?
    a: Hang up and open the Amazon app yourself. If Your Orders shows nothing, nothing happened. Amazon does not call about orders you did not place.

Amazon impersonation works because almost everyone has an account and almost everyone is waiting for a package. The account itself is easy to secure; the harder part is the habit: every message about Amazon is checked inside the real app, never by clicking or calling back.
