notice: CP14
title: "IRS Notice CP14: you owe a balance"
meaning: "A CP14 is the IRS's first bill: it says you filed a return that shows tax due and the amount was not paid in full."
why: "Your return showed a balance due (or the IRS adjusted it) and the payment was short, late or missing."
deadline: "Pay or respond by the date on the notice, usually 21 days from the notice date, to limit additional penalties and interest."
ifIgnored: "Interest and the failure-to-pay penalty keep accruing, and the IRS sends CP501, CP503 and CP504 in turn, the last of which announces intent to levy."
doThisWeek:
  - "Compare the notice to your filed return; if you agree, pay what you can at irs.gov/payments."
  - "If you cannot pay in full, set up a payment plan online before the deadline; balances under $50,000 are approved immediately."
  - "If you disagree, call the number on the notice with your return and proof of payment in hand."
  - "Check irs.gov/account to confirm the balance is real; CP14 letters are also faked by scammers."
getHelpWhen:
  - "You have unfiled years as well as this balance."
  - "The balance is more than you could pay within 72 months."
  - "The notice is for a year you believe you already paid."
related: [cp501, cp504]
published: 2027-02-15
updated: 2027-02-15
---

The notice number is printed in the top right corner of the letter. Match it before doing anything else; scammers mail fake IRS letters, and a real one can be confirmed at irs.gov/account, where the same balance will appear.
