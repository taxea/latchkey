meaning: "An LT11 (or Letter 1058) is the IRS's final warning before it levies your wages, bank accounts or other assets, and it gives you 30 days to request a Collection Due Process hearing."

ifIgnored: "The IRS can garnish wages, take money from bank accounts and seize other assets without further notice, and you lose the right to a hearing."

The notice number is printed in the top right corner of the letter. Match it before doing anything else; scammers mail fake IRS letters, and a real one can be confirmed at irs.gov/account, where the same balance will appear.
