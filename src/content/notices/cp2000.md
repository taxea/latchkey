---
notice: CP2000
title: "IRS Notice CP2000: proposed changes because income did not match"
meaning: "A CP2000 is not a bill; it is a proposal to change your return because income reported to the IRS by employers, banks or brokers does not match what you filed."
why: "A W-2, 1099 or other form reported income or a transaction that was missing or different on your return."
deadline: "Respond by the date on the notice, usually 30 days, agreeing, partly agreeing or disagreeing with documentation."
ifIgnored: "The IRS assesses the proposed tax, penalties and interest and sends a statutory notice of deficiency (CP3219A), after which your options narrow to Tax Court."
doThisWeek:
  - "Compare the IRS's list of reported income to your records; the mismatch is often a 1099 you forgot or a stock sale where the cost basis was not reported."
  - "If the IRS is right, sign the response form and pay or set up a plan."
  - "If it is wrong or partly wrong, respond in writing with documents (for example, the cost basis of shares sold) before the deadline."
  - "Do not file an amended return in response; use the CP2000 response process."
getHelpWhen:
  - "The proposed amount is large and involves stock, crypto or business income."
  - "You cannot reconstruct the records."
  - "A CP3219A has already arrived."
related: [lt11, cp14]
published: 2027-02-15
updated: 2027-02-15
---

The notice number is printed in the top right corner of the letter. Match it before doing anything else; scammers mail fake IRS letters, and a real one can be confirmed at irs.gov/account, where the same balance will appear.
