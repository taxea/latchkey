title: "IRS Notice CP503: second reminder, balance still due"
meaning: "A CP503 is the second reminder that a balance is unpaid; it is the last routine reminder before the IRS announces its intent to levy."
why: "Two earlier notices (CP14 and CP501) went unanswered."
deadline: "Respond by the date on the notice, usually about 10 days."
ifIgnored: "The next notice is CP504, intent to levy, and the IRS may file a federal tax lien."
doThisWeek:
  - "Set up a payment plan or pay today at irs.gov; the online agreement stops the escalation."
  - "If you cannot pay, call the number on the notice and ask about hardship (Currently Not Collectible) options."
  - "Confirm every return is filed; the IRS will not approve a plan until they are."
getHelpWhen:
  - "The balance includes a year you did not file (a Substitute for Return)."
  - "You have received any letter mentioning a levy or lien."
related: [cp501, cp504]
published: 2027-02-15
updated: 2027-02-15
---

The notice number is printed in the top right corner of the letter. Match it before doing anything else; scammers mail fake IRS letters, and a real one can be confirmed at irs.gov/account, where the same balance will appear.
