The texts started Monday morning and were reported by readers from San Diego to Sacramento by Tuesday. Several versions name a specific bridge or toll road; the link domains change every few hours. None of them is FasTrak.

Toll agencies in California contact drivers by mail to the vehicle's registered address, or through the account you created. A text with a payment link is a scam every time. [The full scam record](/scams/toll-text-scam/) has the red flags and what to do if you paid.
