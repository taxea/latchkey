**Item 1: the toll text.** "FasTrak: unpaid toll $6.99, pay by today to avoid a $50 fee." It went to a few million phones this week, including a lot of yours. It is not FasTrak, or E-ZPass, or SunPass; toll agencies bill by mail to the car's registered address. The link is a copy of their site that takes your card number. [What it looks like and what to do if you paid.](/scams/toll-text-scam/)

**Item 2: the setting.** iPhone: Settings, Apps, Messages, Filter Unknown Senders. Android: Messages, menu, Spam protection. Texts from numbers not in your contacts go to a separate list with links disabled. Your mom's phone needs this more than yours. [The full ten-minute iPhone lockdown.](/protect/iphone/)
